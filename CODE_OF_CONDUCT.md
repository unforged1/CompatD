# Code of Conduct

CompatD follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies to the repository, the issue tracker, pull requests and any other project space, and to people representing the project elsewhere.

## In short

* Be respectful and patient. Many people reporting problems are new to Linux.
* Criticise code and ideas, not people.
* No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or personal attacks.
* Don't publish other people's private information without their permission. This includes details in logs and hardware reports.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 is the authority. This summary doesn't replace it.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, email **unforged.crl@gmail.com** with what happened, where, and links or screenshots if you have them. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer will review every report and may respond with a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
